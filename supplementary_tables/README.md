# Machine-readable supplementary tables

Shuaidong Gao, Chongqing Institute of Foreign Studies.

Companion to *CAGate: Cluster-Aware Gating and Cross-Cancer Consensus for Causal
Discovery in Heterogeneous Cancer Transcriptomes*.

These five workbooks carry the printed tables and the quantities of Notes SN20,
SN25 and SN26 in machine-readable form. They are the workbooks named in the
*Data files* note of the Supplementary Material. Every value is checked against
the typeset text and tables.

| Workbook | Sheets | Backs |
|:--|:--|:--|
| `TableS1-S4_data_and_benchmarks.xlsx` | `S1_TCGA_cancer_types`, `S2_Synthetic_benchmark`, `S3_BRCA_dimension`, `S4_Alpha_sensitivity` | Supplementary Tables S1-S4 |
| `TableS5-S6_pan_cancer_results.xlsx` | `S5_Pancancer_33`, `S6_Directed_TRRUST` | Supplementary Tables S5-S6 |
| `Fig2-Fig3_underlying_data.xlsx` | `Fig2ab_edges_support`, `Fig2c_paired_support`, `Fig2d_TRRUST_either`, `Fig3a_cluster_dF1`, `Fig3b_synth_dSHD`, `Fig3c_reclustering`, `FigS1b_real_dimension` | the numbers plotted in Figures 2 and 3 and Supplementary Figure S1(b) |
| `Notes_SN20-SN26_underlying_data.xlsx` | `SN20_axis_split`, `SN20_edge_distribution`, `SN20_panel_coherence`, `SN25_subtype_control`, `SN25_forced_alpha`, `SN26_effect_size`, `SN26_multiplicity` | the supporting quantities of Notes SN20, SN25 and SN26 |
| `Key_results_summary.xlsx` | `Key_results`, `Workbook_index` | the headline numbers of the manuscript, each with its source table |

Each workbook ends with a Notes sheet carrying definitions, units and the
convention used for any two-sided or absolute quantity.

## Where the numbers come from

| Sheet family | Source in this package |
|:--|:--|
| `TableS1-S4`, `S5_Pancancer_33` | `data/pan_cancer/*.json` (the raw 33 per-cancer runs are also in `data/evidence.json`) |
| `S2_Synthetic_benchmark` | `data/synthetic_dimension_sweep/*.json` |
| `S3_BRCA_dimension`, `FigS1b_real_dimension` | `data/real_dim/brca_d{50,100,150,200}.json` |
| `S4_Alpha_sensitivity` | `data/alpha_sweep.json` |
| `Fig2ab_edges_support`, `Fig2c_paired_support` | `data/evidence.json`, `data/pan_cancer/*.json` |
| `Fig2d_TRRUST_either`, `S6_Directed_TRRUST` | `data/dirval_results.json`, `data/tf_enriched/*.json` |
| `Fig3a_cluster_dF1` | `data/cluster_sweep/*.json` |
| `Fig3b_synth_dSHD` | `data/evidence.json` (array `synth`) |
| `Fig3c_reclustering` | `data/cluster_rf/*.json` |
| `SN20_*` | `data/A_M3_axes.json`, `data/A_M3_axes2.json`, `data/figS34_data.json`, `data/edges_{d300,np300}.json`, `data/B_hi_edges_{d300,np300}.json` |
| `SN25_*` | `data/A_M1_subtype.json`, `data/subtype_eval.json`, `data/subtype_final.json`, `data/subtypes_run/*` |
| `SN26_*` | `data/A_M6_effect.json`, `data/table_s5.json` |

The scripts in `scripts/` recompute the quantities in `data/` from the raw inputs;
the workbooks themselves are formatted copies of those numbers and of the printed
tables, and are not generated by `run_all.py`.

## Note on scope

Tables S7-S13 are printed in the Supplementary Material and are not mirrored here
as data sheets, because they are listings of gene sets and edge sets rather than
numeric results; the underlying lists are in `data/`.
